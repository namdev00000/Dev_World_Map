Antarctica: The Antarctic Treaty System

Antarctica has no permanent sovereign countries or indigenous population. It is regulated by the international Antarctic Treaty System (1959), which designates the continent as a scientific preserve, prohibits military activity, and freezes territorial claims. It hosts ~1,000 to 5,000 seasonal researchers across dozens of multinational stations.

Antarctica also features Don Juan Pond, Earth's saltiest body of water (>44% salinity), located in the McMurdo Dry Valleys.
